One-pager for the committee

Written to be forwarded and read cold, by people who have not met you.

Proposal: rebuilding the club website

There is a working preview, built with the club's own content, at stfc-preview.pages.dev. It is best looked at before reading any of this.

What is being proposed

A full rebuild of stfc.org.au. The club keeps every article it has. The content is the same; how members find it is not.

Why, in numbers

All of this was checked against the site itself.

  • The Articles page is about 500 links with nothing to tell them apart. No search, no filter, no descriptions. You can only find an article if you already know its name.
  • No article carries a date, an author, or a topic.
  • 142 web addresses carry a stray "-2" left behind by an old import.
  • 23 articles are published twice, filed under two different sections.
  • The "next article" link means nothing. Air Layering / Marcotting is followed by "Visiting Nguon & Han Kov".

What changes for members

  • Search across the whole library, with results appearing as you type.
  • Filter by topic and by section.
  • A one-line summary under every entry, so a list is worth reading.
  • Related articles at the foot of each page, chosen by subject rather than by the order things were added.
  • Works properly on a phone.

What it costs

  • To build: nothing. Commercially this is $1,500 to $3,000 of work.
  • To run: $20 a month, covering hosting and ongoing upkeep. Worth comparing against what the club pays for WordPress hosting today.
  • In return, the club agrees to a permanent link to treestock.com.au and the Treesmith app in the site footer, and one mention in a club newsletter at launch.

What happens to the site we have now

  • Nothing breaks. Every existing web address redirects to its new home. 162 of those redirects are already written.
  • The content stays the club's. Nothing is added, removed or rewritten.
  • The current site stays up and untouched until the committee says switch.

Who edits it afterwards

Club volunteers, through a simple web editor. No coding, no technical accounts to set up, no knowledge required beyond using a browser. The topic tags and summaries are ordinary text that anyone can correct.

Who is doing the work

Benedict Noel, who already runs:

  • wanatca.org.au, the WA Nut & Tree Crop Association's site and archive.
  • rfcarchives.org.au, the Rare Fruit Council of Australia's archive. About 1,800 pages, moved to new hosting without a single broken link.
  • treestock.com.au, around 3,000 pages tracking fruit tree stock across 27 Australian nurseries.
  • The WA Rare Fruit Club's photo competition voting system.

What happens next

A few weeks of work after a go-ahead, then the switch happens on a date the committee picks. Nothing changes for members until that day.

What the committee is being asked to decide

  1. Whether to go ahead.
  2. Which of the two designs in the preview.
  3. Whether Articles and Tips stay as separate sections.

What the club needs to provide

  • One person as a point of contact.
  • Control of the domain name when it is time to switch, and not before.

If the answer is no

Nothing has been lost. The current site has not been touched.
