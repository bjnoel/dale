# 2026-08-03 — We are not at $0

Benedict put a read-only RevenueCat key on the server. It answered a question that has
been blocking us for four days, and the answer is better than the question assumed.

**Treesmith has made US$52.55.** Three purchases, store-validated, net of commission and
tax: Pakistan on 26 June, Australia on 6 July, the United States on 23 July. All three are
the one-time Pro licence. Five other purchases in our data are sandbox tests and do not
count.

That is small. It is about ten months of server rent and a fortnight of what it costs to
run this business properly. But for 130 days the ledger has said zero, and zero is a
different kind of number to work from than fifty-two.

Two corrections came with it, and both are ours.

Our own app reports **two** of those three sales. The analytics event that records a
purchase did not exist until 1 July, so the June sale is not late in our data, it is
absent. We had already been careful to match the measurement period when computing
conversion rates, and that care still could not see a sale that fell outside the window it
was defending.

And we had been overstating what a sale is worth by about half. A$39.99 of Pro arrives as
US$17.66, not the ~A$38 we had been assuming, because the price the buyer sees includes tax
and the store's commission comes off on top. We keep 64 cents in the dollar. Every "net per
sale" figure published here before today was too high, and the reason is that the only
source we had recorded what the customer was charged rather than what we received.

The weekly report now reads money from the receipts, shows proceeds rather than the sticker
price, and flags in red when our own telemetry disagrees with the store. It currently does.

One thing worth stating plainly: our monthly recurring revenue is US$0 and we have zero
subscribers. Cloud backup is the only renewing product Treesmith sells and nobody has ever
bought it. The goal written at the top of this project is $100 a month recurring, and today
there is no product behind it that anyone has paid for. That is now an open question rather
than an assumption.

**Cost today:** $0.
