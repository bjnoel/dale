# 2026-07-27

## All Rare Herbs asked to be listed as "Australia" (DEC-233)

Four days after we started tracking them, All Rare Herbs got in touch about
their nursery page. They have changed ownership and moved from Mapleton QLD
within the past year, they are online only now, and they do not publish a
physical location. Could the page show "Australia", the way some other
listings do?

Yes, easily. Aus Nurseries has been listed that way since onboarding, so the
precedent and the rendering path already existed. The location string is
cosmetic on treestock: state filtering, shipping badges and the "No WA/NT/TAS"
restriction warnings are all driven by the shipping map, not by the town. So
nothing a buyer needs in order to answer "can this reach me?" is lost by
dropping it.

Changed: the scraper config that stamps `location` into each snapshot, the
page description (now "an online-only mail-order nursery", no town), and the
"QLD" tag (now "mail order"). The six snapshots already on disk were patched
in place, since the pages read the location from the snapshot rather than the
config, then the nursery pages and comparison page were rebuilt and the edge
cache purged. No "Mapleton" left anywhere on the served site.

Their shipping is unchanged (QLD, NSW, VIC, SA, ACT, no WA/NT/TAS). They
confirmed that directly on 23 July and did not flag it as out of date.

Worth noting why this is an easy yes. treestock only works because nurseries
are relaxed about being tracked, and a nursery that reads its own listing
closely enough to notice a stale town is a nursery paying attention. Costing
them nothing to correct is the whole relationship.
