# 2026-07-30 (evening session)

## What I did

Benedict left a one-word comment on DAL-176 ("extend the WA guide to a national version"):
*Investigate this*. I did, and the answer was no.

**The WA Rare Fruit Guide has never been measurable.** Plausible reports 0 pageviews for
`/wa-rare-fruit-guide.html` over 30 days and 0 over its entire six-month life. That is not a
traffic figure. The page carries no analytics script. It was hand-written straight into the
web root on 9 June instead of going through the template that injects the tag on the other
184 pages. So we shipped a lead magnet, linked it from the busiest element on the site, wrote
a ticket asserting that it converts, and never once counted a visit to it.

It was not in version control either. A clean rebuild of the web root would have deleted it
and nothing would have failed.

**It also told WA buyers to order from two nurseries that cannot ship to them.** The guide
named PlantNet and Heritage Fruit Trees as able to ship into Western Australia. Our own
shipping data says neither does, and it omitted two that can (The Diggers Club, Garden
Express) plus a WA citrus specialist (St Clements Citrus). Somebody could have followed our
advice and had an order refused at quarantine.

Fixed and shipped: the guide now lives in version control, carries the analytics tag, lists
the correct nine nurseries that reach a WA address, and includes the finding from yesterday's
availability analysis that WA is not the cut-off state people think it is (105 of 108 species
reachable; Tasmania, at 35 of 108, is the state with that problem).

**Separately, the first ranking of our pages by revenue rather than traffic.** Every outbound
click to a nursery can be attributed to the page it came from, and nobody had ever asked. Over
30 days, 1,245 clicks across 345 pages:

- State-targeted buy pages: **0.45 outbound clicks per visitor**, the best on the site.
- Site average: 0.28.
- Guide and editorial pages: **0.01**. 267 visitors produced 4 clicks.

State pages were already the best page type on search performance. They are now also the best
on the only revenue path this business has, measured independently. Per page built they are
worth about sixteen times a variety page, and roughly 43 more of them would cover the server
bill.

The editorial number is the uncomfortable one. Our second most-read page produces almost no
referral value. Content on this site has to justify itself on links or subscribers, and we
currently measure neither.
